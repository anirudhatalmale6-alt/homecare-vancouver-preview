Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation · Greater Vancouver

Physiotherapy and rehabilitation to support mobility and recovery.

Recovering strength and mobility after an injury, surgery or illness takes consistency. Our caregivers support seniors in following prescribed exercise routines and rehabilitation plans safely, between visits from physiotherapy professionals.

Service overview

Consistency between therapy sessions.

Physiotherapy and rehabilitation programs work best with regular practice — but many seniors need a steady, safe hand to help with exercises and mobility routines between professional sessions.

Our caregivers work alongside your loved one's physiotherapist or rehabilitation team, providing encouragement, safety supervision and consistent support with prescribed exercises and mobility aids, so progress continues between appointments.

Benefits

Why families add rehabilitation support.

Consistency between sessions

Regular practice between physiotherapy visits supports steadier progress.

Safety-focused supervision

Caregivers help reduce fall risk during exercises and mobility practice.

Coordinated care

We can communicate with your loved one's physiotherapist to stay aligned on goals.

Encouragement & motivation

A familiar, supportive presence helps keep recovery routines on track.

Is it time?

Signs rehabilitation support could help.

  • A recent injury, surgery or illness affecting mobility or strength
  • Difficulty remembering or completing prescribed exercises independently
  • Increased fall risk during mobility practice at home
  • A physiotherapist has recommended additional at-home support
  • Family caregivers feel unsure how to safely assist with exercises
  • Progress has stalled without consistent practice between sessions
