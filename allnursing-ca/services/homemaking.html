Homemaking · Greater Vancouver

A tidy, comfortable home, without the strain.

Keeping up with household chores can become genuinely difficult as mobility and energy change. Our homemaking service takes cleaning, laundry and light meal preparation off your loved one's plate, so the home stays safe and comfortable.

Service overview

A well-kept home supports wellbeing.

A cluttered or poorly maintained home isn't just uncomfortable — it can become a genuine safety hazard for a senior with mobility or balance concerns. Our homemaking caregivers handle the everyday upkeep that keeps a home clean, organized and safe.

Support typically includes dusting, vacuuming, laundry, changing bed linens and preparing light meals, fitted around your loved one's routine and reviewed as part of their overall care plan.

Benefits

Why homemaking support helps.

Reduces fall hazards

Clear floors, tidy walkways and fresh linens all support a safer home environment.

Frees up energy for what matters

Seniors can spend time on hobbies and relationships instead of housework.

Consistent, trusted help

The same caregiver becomes familiar with your loved one's home and preferences.

Pairs naturally with other care

Homemaking is easily bundled with personal care or companion visits.

Is it time?

Signs homemaking support could help.

  • Housekeeping tasks are being skipped or left undone
  • Laundry is piling up or bed linens aren't changed regularly
  • Meal preparation has become tiring or inconsistent
  • Clutter or mess is creating fall risks around the home
  • A loved one feels overwhelmed keeping up with daily upkeep
  • Family caregivers are stretched thin trying to manage the household remotely
