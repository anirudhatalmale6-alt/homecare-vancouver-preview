Alzheimer's Care · Greater Vancouver

Quality Alzheimer's care, in the comfort of home.

An Alzheimer's diagnosis changes daily life for the whole family. Our specially trained caregivers provide steady, familiar support that helps clients stay safe, calm and as independent as possible, for as long as possible.

Service overview

Familiar faces, steady routines.

Alzheimer's disease often makes new people and unfamiliar settings especially disorienting. We prioritize consistency — matching clients with familiar caregivers and keeping daily routines steady — to reduce confusion and anxiety wherever possible.

Support ranges from safety supervision and personal care to help with meals, medication reminders and meaningful activities, all guided by a plan a Registered Nurse builds during your free assessment and updates as the condition changes.

Benefits

Why families choose our Alzheimer's care.

Consistency & familiarity

The same caregiver visits whenever possible, easing the disorientation new faces can cause.

Safety-first supervision

Caregivers are trained to reduce wandering risk and other Alzheimer's-related safety concerns.

RN oversight throughout

Care plans are reviewed regularly as needs evolve through different stages.

Support for daily structure

Meals, routines and activities are kept as consistent and calming as possible.

Is it time?

Signs Alzheimer's care could help.

  • Increasing memory loss affecting daily safety or routines
  • Wandering or getting lost in familiar places
  • Difficulty recognizing family members or familiar surroundings
  • Missed medications or difficulty following daily routines
  • Family caregivers are stretched thin managing round-the-clock supervision
  • A recent diagnosis has left the family looking for specialized support
