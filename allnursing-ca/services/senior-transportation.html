Senior Transportation · Greater Vancouver

Transportation for seniors with a familiar face along the way.

Getting to appointments, errands and outings shouldn't be a source of stress. Our caregivers provide safe, accompanied transportation for seniors — with support getting in and out of the vehicle, and companionship the whole way.

Service overview

Independence, without the worry.

Giving up driving doesn't have to mean giving up independence. Our transportation service pairs seniors with a trusted caregiver who provides safe, door-to-door support for appointments, errands and the outings that keep life full.

Caregivers assist with getting in and out of the vehicle, carrying bags or mobility aids, and staying present throughout the appointment or outing if needed — all coordinated as part of your loved one's broader care plan.

Benefits

Why families rely on our transportation support.

Door-to-door assistance

Caregivers help with every step, from leaving home to returning safely.

Support during appointments

Caregivers can stay for medical visits to help relay information to family.

Reduces missed appointments

Reliable transportation means fewer cancelled or postponed medical visits.

Keeps social life active

Regular outings help combat isolation and support overall wellbeing.

Is it time?

Signs transportation support could help.

  • A loved one has stopped driving or driving has become unsafe
  • Missed medical appointments due to transportation challenges
  • Difficulty carrying groceries or navigating errands independently
  • Reduced social outings due to lack of reliable transportation
  • Family members can't always be available to drive a loved one
  • Mobility aids make public transit difficult or unsafe
