Personal Care · Greater Vancouver

Everyday personal care, handled with dignity.

Bathing, dressing, grooming and hygiene routines can become difficult — and deeply personal — as we age. Our caregivers provide this support with patience, discretion and respect, so your loved one feels cared for rather than managed.

Service overview

Support for the routines that matter most.

As everyday tasks like bathing, dressing and grooming become harder to manage alone, small losses of independence can feel like big ones. All Nursing Health Services's personal care service is designed to preserve as much independence and routine as possible, while making sure essential hygiene and comfort needs are never missed.

Our caregivers are trained to provide hands-on assistance with eating and feeding, grooming, dressing, bathing, incontinence care and mobility — always guided by the care plan a Registered Nurse builds during your free in-home assessment, and adjusted as needs evolve.

Benefits

Why families choose our personal care service.

Dignity-first approach

Every task is carried out with discretion and respect, minimizing embarrassment around sensitive routines like bathing and incontinence care.

Consistent, familiar caregivers

We match clients with the same caregiver wherever possible, so routines and preferences don't need to be re-explained each visit.

RN-designed care plans

Personal care is never generic — each plan reflects the specific health, mobility and comfort needs identified during assessment.

Flexible scheduling

From a single morning visit to full daily support, hours can flex around your loved one's routine and your family's needs.

Is it time?

Signs a loved one may need personal care support.

  • Skipping baths or showers, or wearing the same clothes repeatedly
  • Noticeable changes in personal hygiene or grooming habits
  • Difficulty with buttons, zippers or dressing independently
  • Signs of skin irritation from incontinence going unmanaged
  • Unsteadiness or fear of falling during bathing or transfers
  • Family caregivers feeling overwhelmed or burnt out
