Open roles

Current opportunities across Greater Vancouver.

Home Support Worker / Caregiver

Provide personal care, companionship and daily living support to seniors across Greater Vancouver. Full-time, part-time and live-in positions available.

Registered Nurse (RN)

Lead in-home assessments, build and supervise care plans, and provide skilled nursing visits including wound care and medication management.

Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)

Support clinical nursing visits, including IV therapy, wound care and monitoring, under the direction of our RN care team.

Live-In Caregiver

Provide round-the-clock companionship and personal care support for clients seeking continuous, in-home care.

Care Coordinator

Work directly with families and caregivers to schedule visits, coordinate care plans and ensure smooth day-to-day operations.

Nanny / Childcare Provider

Join our nanny placement roster to provide trusted, vetted childcare for families across Greater Vancouver.
