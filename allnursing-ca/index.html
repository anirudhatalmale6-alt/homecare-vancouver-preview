Trusted Nurses and Caregivers Serving Greater Vancouver Since 2005.

All Nursing Health Services Inc. is a trusted Vancouver-based home care and nursing provider dedicated to helping seniors and older adults live safely, comfortably, and independently in their own homes. Whether clients require a few hours of companionship each week or 24/7 skilled nursing care, every personalized care plan is carefully developed, supervised, and regularly monitored by a Registered Nurse to ensure the highest standard of care and provide peace of mind for families.

We offer a comprehensive range of professional home care and nursing services tailored to each client's unique needs. Our services include home nursing care, personal care, live-in caregivers, dementia care, Alzheimer's care, palliative care, IV therapy, wound care, foot care, post-surgery care, rehabilitation services, medication management, respite care, and customized in-home support. Our experienced and compassionate team proudly serves clients throughout Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, West Vancouver, North Vancouver, Coquitlam, Surrey, White Rock, Langley, and surrounding Metro Vancouver communities. We are committed to delivering reliable, high-quality, and compassionate care that enhances quality of life while allowing clients to remain in the comfort and familiarity of their own homes.

Why families choose us

Care that feels like family, backed by clinical standards.

Founded by a Registered Nurse with a Master's in Health Administration, All Nursing Health Services Inc was built on a simple belief: seniors do best when their independence is respected, and their care is never left to guesswork.

RN-supervised, not left to chance

Every care plan begins with a complimentary in-home assessment by a Registered Nurse, then stays under RN oversight for as long as care continues.

Matched, not assigned

Dignity, always

How care begins

Four steps from first call to ongoing care.

Call or request a visit

Reach our care team any hour of the day — no automated menus, just a real conversation about what your family needs.

Free RN assessment

A Registered Nurse visits your loved one at home, at no cost, to understand their health, routines and preferences.

A care plan & caregiver match

We build a personalized plan and match a caregiver whose skills and personality suit your loved one — with backup always ready.

Ongoing RN oversight

Care doesn't stop at day one. An RN continues to review and adjust the plan as needs change, for as long as care is needed.

Clinical oversight, built in

Every plan is designed — and reviewed — by a Registered Nurse.

Home care agencies vary widely in how closely they supervise the care they provide. At All Nursing Health Services, RN oversight isn't an add-on service — it's the foundation of every plan, from the first assessment onward.

Complimentary in-home assessment before care ever begins, with no obligation to proceed.

Ongoing plan reviews as health, mobility or cognition changes over time.

Direct communication with family members and, where appropriate, care facility staff.
