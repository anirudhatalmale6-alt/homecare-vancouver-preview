Home Care Nurse Can Help a Senior With Dementia

Updated 2025-02-01 · 3-minute read

A home care nurse can help a senior with dementia. Home care services for seniors with dementia involve activities such as eating, washing, bathing, dressing, and much more. Dementia is a debilitating disease because it brings down a person’s mental faculties and makes them dependent on others for even the smallest of tasks. They are not in a position to fend for themselves. If there is an elderly family member suffering from dementia in your home, you definitely need professional assistance to take care of them because of the complicated nature of the illness. Hiring a nurse is a good option to provide professional care for your loved one. The need to seek help from professionals is key to deal with the situation when your loved one is suffering from Dementia.

Why do You Need the Assistance of a Nurse?

A qualified nurse can help your loved one with various activities such as eating, washing, bathing, and much more. Trained caregivers have the needed skills to understand how dementia can affect the mind. They can care for and manage the person professionally. Caregivers will look into their every need and provide good attention without which your loved one will succumb to health problems or injuries. Nurses will make sure that your loved one takes their medication regularly so that their health problems do not deteriorate.

When you are a working person it is difficult to spare time for assisting a person suffering from Dementia. A nurse will look after the person while you are away at work and will help them to take medication, bathe, and eat their food. The nurse will talk to them and take them for walks so that they stay active and get physical exercise.

A person suffering from Dementia is susceptible to mood swings. During normal days the person will do all activities well while on bad days the person’s behavior can be hostile. This can be difficult to manage if you have to look after the person yourself. A nurse will know how to manage such behavior so that it passes off peacefully and the person becomes normal again. In case of any physical distress, they know what treatment to give so that the pain subsides and the patient is back to usual self.

A reputable Dementia care agency in Vancouver will provide trained and dedicated nurse so that you’re loved gets excellent medical care during the most difficult phase of their life. The charges for hiring a nurse are reasonable. In addition to being a person who offers treatment and care to your loved one, the nurse is also a counselor and you can turn to them for advice in caring for your loved one. You can opt for the services of a nurse on an hourly basis or full time. The hiring can be customized according to your requirements ranging from a couple of hours every week to round the clock care.
