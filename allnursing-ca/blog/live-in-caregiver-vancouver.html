Live-in Caregiver in Vancouver, BC

Updated 2025-02-01 · 3-minute read

Live in caregiver in Vancouver BC is widely used for seniors who want to stay in their homes. Parents are the only two people in the world whom you can trust and rely upon. No matter what financial, social, or personal trouble you are going through, your parents are your pillars of strength; they are always by your side to care for you. They spend most of their life looking after you and ensuring you never face hardship. However, as they age, the age starts taking its toll on them and they become the one who needs to be looked after. The same is the case with other elder family members. Hiring a live-in caregiver in BC services for elderly loved ones in Vancouver is essential to help the elderly to overcome the limitations that are associated with aging.

Initially, you may think that you will be able to take care of them yourself but gradually your work and personal commitments curb all your time, and you are left with no extra time and energy to devote to their needs. You are then left to choose between two options, either send them to an old age home where many of the elderly reside or hire a private caretaker to care for them.

Going to an old age home is not a happy option for most elderly family members. Though your parents may find many other elderly individuals to mingle with they will always miss your presence. It is a fact that in care facilities, love ones lose their privacy.  They also may face the risk of injury when another resident with dementia unintentionally walks in and out of their rooms. In such cases, confrontation may occur that could lead to shouting and push around that can result in unwanted falls and injuries. They may also be restricted from receiving meals at set times unlike their homes when they have access to meals anytime. Another risk of being in aged institutions is acquiring common respiratory and gastrointestinal outbreaks. In such an environment there are a large number of seniors with a mostly weak immune system who can become ill when only some residents acquire infectious diseases. Taking the help of a live-in caregiver in BC is a better choice. This way their needs will be taken care of and they also have the satisfaction of living in familiar surroundings with having access to other family members.

Hiring a live in caregiver in Vancouver BC also saves you the commute time from your house to the old age home. If the old care home is at a distance from your house then it may not always be feasible for you to go and meet them at regular intervals. Also, it will make them feel like you are beginning to forget them. Love and care are the only two things the elderly expect from their children. Another thing that might bother you against sending them to an old age home facility is the kind of treatment they may receive there. However, if they stay at home, they will always remain in front of your eyes and you will be able to see how they are being treated and cared for by the friendly home care services staff.

While it may not be always possible for you to take out time from your busy schedule and look after your elders, it is possible to arrange for caregivers who would treat them like you would. So if you care for your elders and want to give them the love and respect that they deserve, then hiring a live-in caregiver is the best option for you.
